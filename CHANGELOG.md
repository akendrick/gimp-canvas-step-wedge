# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-12

First release, published at <https://github.com/akendrick/gimp-canvas-step-wedge>
under the MIT License.

- **Colors → Canvas Step Wedge...**: grows the canvas and adds a gray step
  wedge below the image, on its own "Step Wedge" layer, as one undo step.
- 2–40 steps (default 12), from 0% (white) to 100% (black) in even ink-%
  steps across the full image width.
- Box size (default 15px), with the gap and bottom margin matching it by
  default or set separately.
- Black band, outlines with selectable colour (default 50% gray) and width,
  and optional ink-% labels at about 40% of the box height.
